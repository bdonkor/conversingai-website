AI Receptionist FAQ

Everything you need to know about how our AI Receptionist handles calls, books appointments, captures leads, and works around the clock.

📞 Call Handling

How are incoming calls handled?

+

Every call is answered instantly by the AI receptionist using a professional, human-like voice. There is no waiting, no voicemail, and no missed calls.

Do callers need to press buttons or follow menus?

+

No. Callers speak naturally, and the system responds conversationally—just like a real receptionist.

Can multiple calls be handled at once?

+

Yes. The system can manage many calls simultaneously, ensuring every customer is attended to without delay.

🧠 Understanding & Responses

How does the AI understand what callers are saying?

+

It listens to speech and interprets the caller’s intent, allowing it to respond appropriately to requests like bookings, inquiries, or general questions.

Where does it get its answers from?

+

The AI leverages every piece of knowledge available to it—including your specific services, pricing, operating hours, and business policies—to provide accurate, human-like responses just as a business owner would.

Is the conversation natural or robotic?

+

The interaction is designed to feel natural and conversational, closely resembling a human receptionist.

⚙️ Actions & Capabilities

Can it book appointments or reservations?

+

Yes. The AI can check availability and schedule appointments directly or share booking links with customers.

Can it capture customer details?

+

Yes. It collects key information such as name, phone number, and the purpose of the call for follow-up.

Can calls be transferred to a real person?

+

Yes. Calls can be forwarded to you or your team based on rules you set, such as urgency or request type.

Can it answer frequently asked questions?

+

Yes. It can instantly respond to common questions about your services, pricing, availability, and policies.

📊 After the Call

What happens when a call ends?

+

A summary is automatically generated, including caller details, conversation highlights, and any actions taken.

Will I be notified about calls?

+

Yes. You can receive real-time updates, summaries, or alerts for important or missed interactions.

Can it send follow-ups to customers?

+

Yes. The system can send confirmations, booking details, or follow-up messages via SMS or other channels.

⏰ Availability & Reliability

Does it work outside business hours?

+

Yes. The AI receptionist operates 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Will it ever miss a call?

+

No. Every incoming call is answered, ensuring no opportunity is lost.

🚀 Business Impact

How does this improve my business?

+

It ensures every customer is attended to, reduces missed opportunities, and frees up your time by handling repetitive calls.

Is it suitable for my type of business?

+

Yes. It can be adapted to different industries by using your specific business information and workflows.
