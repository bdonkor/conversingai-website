Questions

Will it know our actual policies?
Only what you give it. We train it on your real delivery areas, prices, hours and returns policy. If it has not been told something, it says so rather than inventing an answer.
What happens to the conversations?
They come to you. You see what customers are asking, which is usually the most useful by-product: it shows you what your website should have said in the first place.
What does it cost?
You get a fixed written quote before any work starts. No meter, no surprise at the end. The agent is month to month and you can cancel.
Do I own it?
Yes. The design, the code, the domain and your customer data are yours.
