Fair Questions, Straight Answers

How much does a website cost?
It depends on what the site has to do. A sharp marketing site that wins enquiries is a very different build from a platform with logins, payments and dashboards. Tell us what you need and we send a fixed written quote before any work starts, so there is no meter running and no surprise at the end.
How long does it take?
A focused marketing site is usually ready in two to three weeks. Platforms with accounts, payments or custom workflows take longer, and we agree the timeline with you in writing up front. You will know the launch date before we begin, not after.
Do I own the site when it is finished?
Yes. The design, the code and the domain are yours. We do not hold your site hostage, and you are free to take it elsewhere at any time. Most clients stay because the support is worth having, not because they are locked in.
Can you work with the website I already have?
Often, yes. Sometimes the fastest win is fixing speed, structure and the enquiry path on what you already own rather than starting again. We will tell you honestly which of the two makes more sense for you, even when rebuilding would be the bigger invoice.
What happens after launch?
Hosting, updates, backups, security and monitoring are handled for you. A website is not a painting you hang on the wall, it is a machine that needs maintaining, and we keep ours running the same way we keep our own platforms running.
Will it be found on Google?
Technical SEO is built in from the first day, not sold to you as an upgrade later. Clean structure, fast loading, proper metadata and structured data all ship as standard. If you want ongoing content and ranking work on top of that, we offer it separately.
Do you only work with businesses in Ghana?
No. We are a remote team and we build for clients wherever they are. That said, we know the Ghanaian market unusually well, including mobile money, SMS habits and what it means to design for someone on a handset and a patchy connection.
